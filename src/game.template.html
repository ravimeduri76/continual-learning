The Forgetting Machine

Five learners watch the same clicks through the same frozen CLIP embeddings. Four differ by a single dial; the fifth runs two clocks at once. Then the question changes underneath you — and they come apart in exactly the way the theory says they should.

θₖ ← θₖ − ηₖ·Πₖ[ ∇ℒ(θ; 𝒟ₜ∪ℛₜ) + λₖMₖ(θₖ − aₖ) ]
Embeddings: frozen CLIP RN50 (open_clip, CC12M), PCA-reduced. Nothing is sent anywhere — every learner runs in this tab.
The framework these dials come from: Five Dials of Plasticity.
